Both Sampson and Laub's theory and Moffitt's taxonomy enjoy strong empirical support. Sampson and Laub famously reanalyzed the Glueck longitudinal data, tracking 500 delinquent boys from adolescence into their seventies. Moffitt's work draws heavily on the Dunedin Multidisciplinary Health and Development Study from New Zealand.

S25

Let me close with the most hopeful message of life-course criminology: change is always possible. No matter how long someone has been involved in crime, the right turning point — a supportive relationship, a stable job, a moment of personal resolve — can redirect their trajectory. People can desist at any age.
