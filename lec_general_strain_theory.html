🇰🇷 한국어

S25

If strain and negative emotions are at the root of crime, then effective interventions should include emotional support programs for at-risk youth, anti-bullying initiatives in schools, restorative justice practices that address victims' and offenders' emotions, and community-based counseling services. In short, GST brings emotions back into the center of criminological theory — reminding us that crime is often not a cold, rational calculation, but a hot, emotional reaction to pain, loss, and injustice.
